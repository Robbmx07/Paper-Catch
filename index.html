1Paste the text from your photo
How to copy text from a photo
  1. Open the photo (or point the Camera at the paper).
  2. Tap the Live Text icon (lines in a box), or press and hold on the words.
  3. Tap Copy All, come back here, and tap Paste.

Nothing you paste leaves your phone. Only when you tap Add is that one event sent to Google Calendar.

2Check & add

Dates you paste will show up here as events you can edit.
