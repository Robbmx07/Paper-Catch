# Paper Catch

Turn school flyers, bills and appointment cards into calendar events.

1. Take a photo of the paper, tap **Live Text → Copy All**.
2. Open Paper Catch and tap **Paste**. It finds the dates, times and places.
3. Check each event and tap **Add to Google** (or **.ics** for Apple Calendar).

Everything runs in your browser. Pasted text and events are stored only on your own device; nothing is sent anywhere until you tap Add.

Open it at: https://robbmx07.github.io/Paper-Catch/
